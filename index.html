Restricted Access Notice

The IRMP Knowledge Hub and Dashboards contain protected and confidential information, including Indigenous Knowledge. Access is restricted to IRMP Task Team members only. Content must not be shared, distributed, or reproduced beyond the Task Team without prior authorization.

If partners require briefing materials for their organizations, please contact victoria.fleming-chartier@pc.gc.ca.

About this Knowledge Hub

Welcome to the Integrated Research and Monitoring Program (IRMP) Knowledge Hub.

The IRMP is a co-developed program led in partnership by Athabasca Chipewyan First Nation (ACFN), Mikisew Cree First Nation (MCFN), Fort Chipewyan Métis Nation (FCMN), Nipîy Tu Research and Knowledge Centre (NTRKC), and Parks Canada. The program focuses on understanding and monitoring ecological conditions in the Peace-Athabasca Delta (PAD) to support shared learning and informed decision-making.

This Knowledge Hub is a secure workspace for IRMP Task Team members. It provides access to information that supports monitoring, research, and reporting.

Whether you are a technical expert, decision-maker, or land user, this Hub is designed to help you access the level of detail that meets your needs — from high-level summaries to detailed data and supporting documents.
