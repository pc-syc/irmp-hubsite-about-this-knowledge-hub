Restricted Access Notice

The IRMP Knowledge Hub and Dashboards contain protected and confidential information, including Indigenous Knowledge. Access is restricted to IRMP Task Team members only. Content must not be shared, distributed, or reproduced beyond the Task Team without prior authorization.

If partners require briefing materials for their organizations, please contact victoria.fleming-chartier@pc.gc.ca.

About this Knowledge Hub

This knowledge hub presents the outcomes of the Wood Buffalo National Park World Heritage Site Action Plan. It provides access to dashboards, maps, datasets, and story maps on various ecological assessment indicators, offering insights into the park's ecosystem and supporting conservation efforts.
